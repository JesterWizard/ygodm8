## 2026-06-27 — Fixed ALL stat boosts broken by Harpie Lady 1's GetTypeGroup reset

**Root cause:** `ApplyHarpieLady1WindAtkBoost` in `permanent_effects/harpie_lady_1.c` called `GetTypeGroup(zone->id)`, which internally calls `SetCardInfo(zone->id)`. Since `SetCardInfo` resets `gCardInfo.atk/def` to the card's base values, all field-percent and stage modifications applied earlier in `ApplyFieldZoneStatsToCardInfo` were wiped out before the caller (`sub_80572A8__Replacement` / `sub_805733C__Replacement`) read `gCardInfo.atk` for display. This affected **every** monster on the field — not just WIND ones — because the `GetTypeGroup` call ran before the WIND-attribute early-return check.

**Fix:** Replaced `GetTypeGroup(zone->id) != TYPE_GROUP_MONSTER` with `gCardInfo.type >= TYPE_SPELL`, which is equivalent (all monster type values are < TYPE_SPELL) but does not call `SetCardInfo` and therefore preserves the already-computed stat modifications.

**Files:** `src_custom/permanent_effects/harpie_lady_1.c`

**Outcome:** `make test-cards-build` passes (17/17 tests, ROM links, LynJump patches OK). All stat boosts (Seal of Orichalcos, Hourglass of Life, 30% field boosts) now preserve their modifications through the stat display pipeline.

## 2026-06-27 — Fixed Seal of Orichalcos: 1-stage boost not activating (spellEffect deadlock)

**Worked on:** The Orichalcos field spell could be placed face-down on the backrow but could not be activated — pressing A played `SFX_FORBIDDEN` instead. Root cause: the manifest had `spellEffect: 2` (SPELL_EFFECT_MONSTER), which caused `GetSpellType()` to return something other than `SPELL_TYPE_NORMAL`. `HandlePlayerBackrowAction` only calls `ActivateSpellEffect()` for `SPELL_TYPE_NORMAL` cards, so the custom field spell handler (`TryActivateCustomFieldSpell` inside `ActivateSpellEffect__Body`) was never reached — deadlock.

Fixed by changing `spellEffect` from `2` to `8` (SPELL_EFFECT_YAMI) in the manifest. This makes `GetSpellType` return `SPELL_TYPE_NORMAL`, allowing the activation flow to reach `TryActivateCustomFieldSpell`. The custom handler intercepts before vanilla field spell code, so Yami's DARK/Fiend stat mods are never applied — the Orichalcos uses its own custom field stat mods and stage boost.

**Files:**

**Outcome:** `make test-cards-build` passes (17/17 tests, ROM links, LynJump patches OK). Orichalcos can now be activated from the backrow and applies the 1-stage boost to the controller's monsters.
