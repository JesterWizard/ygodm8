## 2026-06-28 — Fix hyperdrive noise

**Worked on:** Hyperdrive played as pure noise while vanilla BGM worked again.

**Root cause:** After shrinking segments, the music tone allocator ran out of tail slots (209–217) and reused vanilla tone indices 0–14, pointing game tones at hyperdrive DPCM blobs. Custom music also used BGM player 0 (mode 1), which reads tone waves as raw PCM instead of DPCM.

**Fix:**
- Never allocate music tones below index 128; fill gaps in 128–208 then tail 209–217.
- Route custom music through voice player 3 / mode 3 (`m4aSongNumStart`), same as custom VO.
- Cap segments at `M4A_VOICE_MAX_VANILLA_SAMPLES` (~54k); hyperdrive = 24 segments.
- Music trim defaults to off (was inheriting voice trim default).

**Files:** `tools/generate_music.py`, `src_custom/custom_music.c`, `tests/host/test_music.py`, regenerated music assets.

**Outcome:** Tones 129–189; `make all` + music tests pass.

**Open / next:** Playtest Hyperdrive in Music Viewer.

## 2026-06-28 — Fix BGM regression from hyperdrive tone patches

**Worked on:** Hyperdrive custom music silenced all vanilla BGM.

**Root cause:** DPCM tone patches for hyperdrive used tone indices 218, 220, 221. Each tone entry is 12 bytes at `0xAFB2CC + index*12`; index ≥ 218 overlaps `g8AFBD0C` (m4a player table). The patch trailer word `0x00FF00FF` overwrote BGM player 0's `info` pointer (`0x02025840` → `0x00FF00FF`).

**Fix:**
- Cap allocatable tone indices at 217 (`M4A_TONE_INDEX_LAYOUT_MAX`).
- Music allocator prefers slots 209+ after custom voices.
- `apply_lynjump.py` rejects tone patches that would overlap the player table.

**Files:** `tools/generate_voices.py`, `tools/generate_music.py`, `tools/apply_lynjump.py`, `tests/host/test_music.py`

**Outcome:** `make all` passes; player 0 pointer restored; hyperdrive uses tones 209–213.
