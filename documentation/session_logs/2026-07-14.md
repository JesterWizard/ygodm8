## Fixed shifted sub-menu: clear loop destroyed text, option 2 glyphs missing

**Bug 1 — Clear loop erased shifted text at rows 11-14:** `TrunkSubMenu_RenderVanillaShifted` wrote shifted text to rows 9-14, then immediately cleared rows 11-16 with a second loop. This wiped out "Move to Deck" (option 1 at rows 11-12) and "Return to Trunk" (option 2 at rows 13-14), leaving only "Details" (row 9-10) visible. **Fix:** Removed the clear loop entirely — the shifted write loop fully owns all 6 rows, and the baked tilemap at rows 15-16 is already blank.

**Bug 2 — No glyph data for option 2 tiles 121-140:** Vanilla `CopyStringTilesToVRAMBuffer` loads only 60 characters (tiles 1-120). Option 2 at tiles 101-140 needed tiles 121-140 loaded. **Fix:** Added `CopyStringTilesToVRAMBuffer(&gBgVram.cbb1[3232], kReturnToTrunkLabel, 0x900)` to load the "Return to Trunk" glyphs at tile 101.
