## Fixed: synchro frame palette overwritten by extended art palette

**Root cause:** The call order in `print_card.c` is `sub_80267E0()` then `CopyCardArtDataToBuffers()`.

## Fixed: custom mini card border invisible — revert to 8bpp + add transparent edge pixels

**SUPERSEDED** by the index-159 fix above. The transparent-edge theory was wrong: vanilla outer rings are index 159, not 0. Zeroing the edge removed the black outline.

**Root cause (wrong initial analysis):** The vanilla mini card LZ data decompresses to **576 bytes (8bpp tiled format)**, not 288 (4bpp). The `sub_80565F0` composition function reads from the 8bpp art buffer at specific byte offsets, interleaving border tile data with art bytes. Our generated art filled the full 24×24 canvas with non-zero pixel values, but the vanilla art has palette-index-0 pixels (transparent) at the outermost ~2px ring. Without those zeros, the art overwrites the border tiles at the overlay positions.

**Fix (two parts):**
1. Reverted back to `.8bpp` intermediate format in `build_mini_lz_from_png` and `build_mini_assets` (the vanilla format is 576-byte 8bpp tiled, not 4bpp).
2. In `make_paletted_mini`, after quantization and remapping, zero out the outermost 2-pixel ring of the 24×24 image (palette index 0 = transparent). This lets the border tiles from `gUnk_8E17F48[color]` show through at the card edge.
